# Bow River Brewing Co. - Plan

## Client
Bow River Brewing is a small craft brewery in Calgary. They need a one page site
to show their beers, get people to come to the taproom, and take inquiries for
tours and events.

## Who is it for
- Adults (18+) in Calgary who like craft beer
- Tourists in Calgary or going to the mountains
- People planning a group tour or a private event

## Goals
1. Show the beers
2. Make the hours and location easy to find
3. Get tour and event inquiries through the contact form

## Sections
1. Home
2. About
3. Beers
4. Tasting Flight
5. Tours & Events
6. Visit Us
7. Contact

The nav bar only links to sections on the same page.

## Features
1. Age check - popup asking if you're 18 or older before you can see the site
2. Beer filter - buttons to filter the beers by style (lager, IPA, stout etc.)
3. Tasting flight - pick up to 4 beers and they show up in your flight
4. Contact form - sends to the server with POST /inquiry, server checks the data
   and sends back a success or error message. Saves to a file and logs in console

## Tools
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js, Express, npm, GitHub, Render
